Case Study — Market Research Brief

Same technology,
three different starting lines.

A research-based prioritization framework for sequencing vehicle-grid integration across Germany, France, and the UK, built from public regulatory and market data, not assumptions.

Scope: Germany · France · UK Method: Public research, structured framework Status: Independent analysis, June 2026
01 — Problem Statement

Three markets. Not equal effort, not equal timing.

The Mobility House's VGI platform can expand into Germany, France, and the UK, but not with equal effort everywhere at once. Each market sits at a different point on regulation, infrastructure readiness, and commercial proof, so a single global launch plan risks moving too early where the ground isn't ready, or too late where the market's own momentum has already been captured.

The problem this brief addresses: which market should get product and partnership investment first, and on what evidence, not instinct, is that decision defensible.

This is independent research using public sources, not internal company data. Treat the framework as a starting structure, not a verdict.

03 — Market Research

Three markets, three different leads

Rated across three axes: how clear the regulation is, how ready the infrastructure and vehicle compatibility are, and how much real commercial traction already exists. Ratings reflect a synthesis of public reporting, not a precise score.

Germany

Largest fleet, newest green light
Regulatory Readiness Newly cleared
Infrastructure & Compatibility Early / fragmented
Commercial Traction Launching 2026

France

Already live, but slow per vehicle
Regulatory Readiness Workable, heavy process
Infrastructure & Compatibility Narrow but proven
Commercial Traction Live today
  • Renault, Mobilize, and The Mobility House already run a live, bundled V2G service in France, launched 2024.— The Mobility House newsroom, Oct 2024

  • V2G still requires formal Enedis authorization and a certified meter, a 3–6 month process per connection.— Enedis smart-charging report; French V2G practitioner guides, 2026
  • Renault-Nissan-Mitsubishi's EVVE pilot targets an 8MW virtual battery across 3 regions by 2027.— EVVE project coverage, 2026

United Kingdom

Original co-launch market, ahead on fees & meters
Regulatory Readiness Fees & meters resolved
Infrastructure & Compatibility Deep pilots, narrow fit
Commercial Traction Live retail product
  • Named alongside France and Germany from the original 2023 Mobilize/TMH partnership announcement, planned for launch in 2025.— Mobilize / TMH partnership announcement, 2023

05 — What This Means for Sequencing

The right answer depends on what you're optimizing for

If optimizing for lowest execution risk
→ France

A live, bundled commercial model already exists. Scaling here means replicating and refining a known playbook, not inventing one.

If optimizing for regulatory tailwind
→ UK, alongside France

Both markets had already resolved the two biggest V2G barriers, double fees and smart meters, while Germany was still catching up. The UK was named a co-launch market from the start.

If optimizing for long-term scale
→ Germany

The largest fleet by far, but infrastructure and DSO fragmentation suggest a partner-led entry rather than a direct one, at least initially.

06 — Defining Success

How we'd know the sequencing call was right

Having looked at the evidence, it's worth naming what "this was the right call" would actually look like six to twelve months later, before moving to what this brief still can't answer.

Time to First Live Deployment
Execution speed

How long from the decision to a real, paying customer live in that market.

Confirmed Partner Agreements
Market access

OEM, energy supplier, or grid-operator agreements signed within the first two quarters.

Regulatory Milestones Met on Schedule
Risk tracking

Whether the market's own regulatory timeline, MiSpeL, Enedis approvals, Ofgem rules, actually holds.

Customer Enrollment Rate
Demand validation

Fleet or driver sign-up in the first 90 days post-launch, the earliest real signal the sequencing bet paid off.

07 — What I'd Still Need to Know

This framework has a ceiling

Public research can map the external landscape. It can't see internal reality, and a real prioritization call needs both.

  • ?What existing OEM and dealer relationships does the company already have in each market?
  • ?Is the France integration work reusable, or does each market need its own build?
  • ?What does current customer demand or pipeline actually look like, market by market?
  • ?Has the UK launch, planned since 2023 alongside France, actually gone live, and if not, what's holding it?
08 — Why I Built This

I wanted to test whether the way I approach ambiguous, multi-sided decisions, gathering real evidence, making the trade-offs explicit, and being honest about what I still don't know, holds up in a domain I'm new to. I've run this kind of structured prioritization before: steering the selection, RFQ, and multi-year delivery of an external development partnership, and leading discovery for a full product rebuild by identifying and validating new use cases before committing to build.

This brief is a genuine first pass, not a final answer. I'd welcome the chance to pressure-test it against what the team already knows.
